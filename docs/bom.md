## Must be ordered

- **6.144MHz** (X1)
- **2x8 BUS** (J3, J4, J7)

## Sockets

Use DIP sockets for every IC (all stocked): 8-pin for the TL072s,
the LM311s and the oscillator can, 14-pin for the 74HC04 / 74HC74 /
74HCT132, 16-pin for the 74HC157 / 74HC4040 / 74HC4049, 20-pin for
the 74HC244.

## The stacking bus

There are no ribbons. Every board carries the SAME 2x8 on 2.54 mm
at the SAME coordinates, and the four boards plug into each other
on 11 mm standoffs:

| Board | Ref | Ways | Position in the stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| digital | J4 | 2x8 | top |
| channel L | J7 | 2x8 | middle |
| channel R | J7 | 2x8 | middle |
| power | J3 | 2x8 | bottom |

Odd pins are all GND, so every signal has a grounded neighbour
either side -- which is what MCLK needs, its jitter being what sets
this converter's noise floor. The even pins carry, in order:
**+5V, VREF_P, VREF_N, MCLK, PUMP, QL, QR, -5V**.

What goes in the sixteen holes is a build choice, not an artwork
choice: a plain socket on the top board, a long-pin header on the
bottom one, and a pass-through stacking header on the two in the
middle. All three are the same sixteen pads. You need 4 sets plus
16 x 11 mm M3 standoffs and the M3 screws; the shop carries none of
them, so they go on the same order as the oscillator can.

## Which channel is which

Both channel boards are the same copper, so nothing etched on them
says which channel a board is. The Q-select shunt does: **J21 1-2
makes it LEFT, 2-3 makes it RIGHT**. Fit one jumper per channel
board and they are otherwise interchangeable.
