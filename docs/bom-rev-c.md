# Bill of materials - discrete delta-sigma vinyl ADC

Generated by `hardware/kicad/tools/make_bom.py` straight from the
schematic, with every line checked against the DTU shop stock list
(`dtu_component_shop.csv`). **ORDER** means the shop does not carry it.

111 components in 43 distinct lines, for the ONE fabricated rev C board (`hardware/kicad/rev_c`), which replaces the four milled boards.

**Board** is always **C**: one four-layer artwork, built once, so a quantity is simply the count on the sheet. The right channel carries the reference sheet's refdes, forty higher than the left. Against the milled boards this adds U2 as an LM358 (docs/design-notes.md 10b'), FB1 on the 5 V inlet, R12/R13 in series with BCLK/LRCLK, and the Pi's own 2x20 socket J2 in place of the pigtail and stacking bus.

## Must be ordered

- **FB** (FB1)
- **6.144MHz** (Y1)
- **74HCU04** (U9)
- **RASPBERRY PI 4** (J2)

## Sockets

Use DIP sockets for every IC (all stocked): 8-pin for the TL072s
and the LM311s, 14-pin for the 74HC04 / 74HCU04 / 74HC74 /
74HCT132, 16-pin for the 74HC157 / 74HC4040 / 74HC4049, 20-pin for
the 74HC244. The crystal Y1 solders in directly.

J2 is a 2x20 FEMALE header on the copper side (a plain 8.5 mm HAT socket; an 11 mm extended-height one if the Pi's PoE header touches the leads at the socket's top end). FB1 is any axial ferrite bead on a lead, 10.16 mm pitch -- or a wire link. The board also wants four M2.5 standoffs for the Pi and four M3 for the enclosure.
