# Bill of materials - discrete delta-sigma vinyl ADC

Generated by `hardware/kicad/tools/make_bom.py` straight from the
schematic, with every line checked against the DTU shop stock list
(`dtu_component_shop.csv`). **ORDER** means the shop does not carry it.

177 components in 65 distinct lines, for the ONE fabricated rev D board (`hardware/kicad/rev_d`): the converter as a Raspberry Pi HAT.

**Board** is always **D**: one four-layer artwork, built once, so a quantity is simply the count on the sheet. The right channel carries the reference sheet's refdes, forty higher than the left. Against rev C this adds the +5VA analog island (L2, C18, C19), a second charge-pump driver (U10), R14 in MCLK, eight status LEDs with their drivers (U12 LM339 clip detectors, Q1 clock-alive pump, Q2/Q3), two buttons, the HAT ID EEPROM (U11, optional), the chassis-ground terminal and lift network (J3, J4), and three test-point headers; L1 replaces rev C's ferrite bead with a stocked choke.

## Sockets

Use DIP sockets for every IC (all stocked): 8-pin for the TL072s
and the LM311s, 14-pin for the 74HC04 / 74HCU04 / 74HC74 /
74HCT132, 16-pin for the 74HC157 / 74HC4040 / 74HC4049, 20-pin for
the 74HC244. The crystal Y1 solders in directly.

Also 8-pin for the LM358 and the 24LC32, 14-pin for the LM339. J2 is a 2x20 FEMALE header on the copper side (a plain 8.5 mm HAT socket; an 11 mm extended-height one if the Pi's PoE header touches the leads at the socket's top end). L1 and L2 are the shop's 10 uH 3 A choke on a 15.24 mm axial footprint. The LEDs are 5 mm, colours as in the Value column; the buttons are 6 mm tactile. Four M2.5 standoffs (11 mm) carry the Pi under the board, four M3 the board in its enclosure.
