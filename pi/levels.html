Last 30 seconds

White dashes are the −6 dBFS target, red is −3 dBFS where the loop goes unstable. Spikes through the red line are clicks in the groove — expected, and the input clamp is built for them. The music is what has to stay under.

How to use this

  1. Play the loudest record you own, somewhere in the middle of a side.
  2. Turn RV20 on the left channel board until the big number reads −6 dBFS and the line says On target.
  3. Do the same on the right board, then let a minute pass and check the match below.
  4. Match by reading, not by rotation — these are ±20 % parts and identical positions differed by about a decibel last time.
  5. Release the hold when you are done, or the ripper will not start a side.

The tuning number is the 95th percentile of the 100 ms peaks, not the maximum: trim to a click and every record ends up ten decibels too quiet.
